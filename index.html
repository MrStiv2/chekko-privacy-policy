Chekko: Money & Receipt Buddy
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy describes how Chekko: Money & Receipt Buddy ("Chekko", the "app") for iPhone and Android, published by CampStudio ("we", "us"), handles information. Contact: campstudiomail@gmail.com.

1. No account, data stays on your device

Chekko works without registration, sign-in or a cloud account. We do not operate a synchronization server and the app contains no behavioural analytics. Everything you record — accounts, transactions, transfers, receipts (items, photos, recognized text and generated PDFs), categories, rules, budgets, plans, subscriptions, goals, inbox items, exchange-rate cache and settings — is stored on your device in an encrypted database (SQLCipher-compatible encryption with a random 256-bit key kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore). The queue of automatically captured payments is encrypted separately (AES-GCM).

Chekko excludes its data from operating-system cloud backups and device-to-device transfers (iCloud/iTunes backup on iOS, Google backup on Android). As a result, your data is not copied anywhere unless you create a Chekko backup yourself (see section 6).

2. What leaves your device and when

The Chekko AI service is operated by CampStudio through its own server (no AI provider keys are stored in the app). To produce results it may rely on third-party AI model providers acting on our behalf. Data sent to it is used to return the requested result (recognized receipt items, imported operations, an answer or a category). Please do not include card numbers or other payment details in questions or photos.

3. Advertising, consent and identifiers

The free version shows banner ads and optional rewarded videos (watching a full video grants an extra AI request) through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Before ads are requested, consent is collected where required through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your choice at any time in Chekko Settings › Ad privacy options (shown while you do not have Premium).

Chekko requests non-personalized ads with a maximum content rating of "G". Your financial data — transactions, amounts, merchants, categories, receipts — is never shared with advertisers or used for ad targeting. Google's SDK may still collect, for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention and diagnostics:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it;
  • device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version);
  • device identifiers — on Android, the Google advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device settings; on iOS, Chekko does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not made available;
  • ad interactions (ads viewed and tapped) and crash or performance diagnostics.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. With Chekko Premium, ads are not loaded at all.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Chekko Premium (monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription) is sold and billed by Apple (App Store) on iOS and Google Play on Android. Purchases are verified on the device (StoreKit 2 / Google Play Billing); we do not run a purchase server and never receive your payment-card or bank details. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account or Google Play subscription settings. Premium is tied to your store account, so it is restored with "Restore purchases" and is not included in Chekko backups.

5. Automatic payment capture

Android (Google Pay / Google Wallet). If you turn on payment capture and grant Notification access, Chekko reads payment notifications from Google Wallet on your phone only. From those notifications it keeps just the amount, currency, merchant and card name, encrypted on the device. All other notifications are ignored and never stored, and nothing from them is sent anywhere. You can revoke Notification access in Android settings at any time.

iPhone (Apple Pay). Chekko has no direct access to Apple Wallet. Capture works only if you create an automation in Apple's Shortcuts app that passes a transaction's amount and merchant to the Chekko action "Add payment to inbox". These values are stored in an encrypted queue on your iPhone and are not sent anywhere. You can delete the automation at any time.

Payment notifications shown by Chekko do not display amounts or merchants; those appear only inside the app.

6. Backups you control

"Export backup" creates a single .chekko file encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256-GCM, key derived with PBKDF2). You decide where to save or share it (Files, a drive, a messenger, etc.). We never receive your backups or passwords and cannot recover a forgotten password. "Restore from backup" replaces the data on the device with the contents of the file.

7. Permissions

Chekko asks for permissions only when a feature needs them: camera and photo library (scanning or choosing receipt photos; on Android scanning uses Google Play services' document scanner), notifications (payment notifications and subscription/income reminders), Notification access on Android (Google Pay capture), and Face ID / Touch ID / device biometrics for the optional app lock (biometric checks are performed by the operating system; Chekko never receives biometric data).

8. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade personal information, and we do not share your financial records with anyone. The only third parties involved are those listed above (exchange-rate providers, the Chekko AI service and its model providers, Google for ads and consent, and Apple/Google for purchases), each only for the purpose described.

9. Children

Chekko is a general personal-finance app and is not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age required by law in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us (for example by email), contact us and we will delete it.

10. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › Backup and data › Delete all data permanently erases accounts, transactions, receipts and photos, categories, plans, inbox, learned categories and settings, turns off app lock and clears payment capture and reminders (you confirm by typing "DELETE").
  • Uninstalling Chekko removes its data from the device. Because Chekko data is excluded from system cloud backups, no copy remains with us.
  • Backup files you exported remain wherever you saved them until you delete them.
  • Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google; deleting app data does not cancel a subscription.
  • Data processed by Google (ads) or Apple/Google (purchases) is governed by their privacy controls.

11. Security

Local data is encrypted with keys held in the platform's secure storage; network requests use HTTPS. Optional app lock (Face ID / Touch ID / passcode, or Android biometrics / screen lock) protects the app, its content is hidden in the app switcher, and amounts can be hidden on screen. No method is perfectly secure, so keep your device and backup passwords protected.

12. Contact and privacy requests

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it only to respond. Please do not send financial records, backup passwords or payment details. Contact campstudiomail@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. Because we do not hold your ledger, most requests are handled by the in-app controls described above.

13. Changes

We may update this policy when the app's functionality or service providers change. The date above identifies the current version. This policy applies to Chekko only; other CampStudio products have separate policies.
